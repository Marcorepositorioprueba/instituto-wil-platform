Unit 1: This is me

15 Hours
1. Introduce oneself with basic personal info. 2. Interact to fill forms. 3. Describe people/places with 'be'.
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Greetings Sugg. greetings to teach: Good morning/afternoon/evening/night, Hello/Hi, What’s up?, How’s it going?, How are you?, I am doing fine/Not so good/So so.

• The Alphabet Letters A to Z.
A: Look at the card. What letter is it? B: It's "u". A: How do you spell “umbrella”? B: U-M-B-R-E-L-L-A.
  • Greeting/Responding in different contexts.
  • Reciting all alphabet letters.
  • Identifying letters in non-alphabetical order.
  • Saying words with random letters.
  • Spelling short/long words.
  • 1.1 greet people in different contexts accordingly,
  • 1.2 respond to greetings appropriately,
  • 1.3 recite all alphabet letters properly,
  • 1.4 identify letters randomly and accurately,
  • 1.5 spell words while having brief oral exchanges.
• Days & School Subjects Days: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun.
Subjects: math, science, social studies, PE, language, English.
✓ I have math on Mondays. ✓ I have English on Wednesdays.
  • Naming/Pronouncing days accurately.
  • Ordering days chronologically.
  • Recognizing subjects from pictures.
  • Describing schedules by mentioning subjects taken.
  • 1.6 pronounce the days accurately,
  • 1.7 arrange days chronologically,
  • 1.8 identify school subjects in visual aids,
  • 1.9 describe school schedules accordingly.

Unit 2: Countries of the world

15 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Nationalities Australia-Australian, Chile-Chilean, China-Chinese, Colombia-Colombian, El Salvador-Salvadoran, England-English, Japan-Japanese, Peru-Peruvian.
A: What’s the nationality of a person from Peru? B: It’s Peruvian. A: How do you spell it? B: P-E-R-U-V-I-A-N.
  • Naming countries on maps.
  • Spelling countries/nationalities.
  • Using capital letters correctly.
  • Having brief conversations to spell origins.
  • 2.1 identify countries from illustrations,
  • 2.2 use capital letters accurately,
  • 2.3 pronounce nationalities adequately,
  • 2.4 spell through short oral exchanges.
• Colors & Souvenirs Black, white, beige, orange, red, purple, brown, blue, green, silver, gold, pink.
Souvenirs: cap, hat, mug, keychain, pen, T-shirt, lanyard, sticker, postcards.
  • Describing flags by telling their color.
  • Inquiring about flag colors.
  • Identifying souvenirs from texts/audios.
  • Matching souvenirs with pictures.
  • 2.5 spell colors orally/writing accurately,
  • 2.6 provide flag descriptions by colors,
  • 2.8 recognize souvenirs from input.

Unit 3: My leisure time

18 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Free-time activities play basketball, soccer, video games, watch TV, listen to music, surf Internet, spend time with family, go shopping, do exercise.

• Simple Present (I, You, We, They) Affirmative: Subject + verb + complement.
✓ I watch TV after school. ✓ They read comics in their free time.
  • Brainstorming activities orally/writing.
  • Following repetition drills.
  • Matching images to words.
  • Building sentences following patterns.
  • Sharing info to find mutual interests.
  • 3.1 name free-time activities from illustrations,
  • 3.3 write complete sentences describing activities,
  • 3.4 exchange info in short conversations.
• Simple Present (He, She, It) Spelling Rules: 1. drink-drinks. 2. fly-flies. 3. pass-passes, match-matches, brush-brushes, fix-fixes. 4. go-goes, do-does, have-has.
  • Applying spelling rules for 3rd person.
  • Making affirmative sentences.
  • Identifying verb ending sounds /ɪz/, /s/, /z/.
  • Pronouncing final -s following rules.
  • 3.9 identify spelling rules successfully,
  • 3.10 spell verbs in 3rd person accurately,
  • 3.12 discriminate sounds /ɪz/, /s/, /z/ in verbs.

Unit 4: My family's and friends’ habits

15 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Family & Possessives grandparents, parents, mother, father, child-children, son, siblings, brother, uncle, cousins.
Possessives: my, your, his, her, its, our, their.
A: Who is she? B: She is my mother. A: Is he your brother? B: Yes, he is.
  • Naming nuclear/extended family.
  • Matching family members with illustrations.
  • Expressing possession with adjectives.
  • Inquiring about relationships in a family tree.
  • 4.1 identify family from different input,
  • 4.2 spell family-related words accurately,
  • 4.3 show possession accordingly,
  • 4.5 exchange info regarding relationships.
• Adverbs of Frequency always, often, usually, sometimes, hardly ever, never.
Pattern: Subject + adverb + verb + complement.
✓ I always go to sleep at 9:00 p.m. ✓ They hardly ever arrive late.
  • Recognizing adverbs from graphics/texts.
  • Matching adverbs with regularity levels.
  • Forming sentences with right word order.
  • Forming "How often...?" questions.
  • 4.9 identify frequency adverbs satisfactorily,
  • 4.11 build short sentences using adverbs,
  • 4.12 inquire about frequency of habits.

Unit 5: My vacation

18 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Time & Holidays Months (Jan-Dec). Ordinals (1st-31st).
Holidays: New Year, Valentine’s, Holy Week, Mother’s Day, Independence Day, Christmas.
A: When is your birthday? B: My birthday is on April 16th.
  • Identifying months in a calendar.
  • Spelling ordinals orally/writing.
  • Ordering months chronologically.
  • Relating holidays to dates.
  • Using prepositions in/on for dates.
  • 5.2 spell months accurately,
  • 5.4 spell ordinal numbers accurately,
  • 5.7 recognize celebrations from visuals,
  • 5.8 mention dates using proper word order.
• Present Continuous Pattern: Subject + be + verb-ing + complement.
Spelling -ing: 1. Add -ing (eat-eating). 2. Verbs ending in -e (dance-dancing). 3. CVC verbs (sit-sitting).
✓ I'm playing soccer. ✓ She is eating cake.
  • Recognizing affirmative components.
  • Describing actions in progress using pictures.
  • Building negative sentences/questions.
  • Writing vacation postcards.
  • 5.9 identify affirmative components,
  • 5.11 describe actions in progress orally,
  • 5.14 make negative statements/questions,
  • 5.16 write coherent vacation descriptions.

Unit 6: My talents

15 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Abilities dance, play instrument, sing, cook, paint, swim, climb a tree, ride a horse, milk a cow.

• Can / Can’t Affirmative: Subject + can + verb. Negative: Subject + can’t + verb.
✓ I can play the guitar. ✓ César can ride a horse. ✓ My sister can't sing.
  • Naming abilities and associating with words.
  • Expressing ability using can/can’t.
  • Listing activities they can/cannot do.
  • Building Yes/No questions with can.
  • 6.1 name abilities accurately,
  • 6.4 state ability or lack of ability,
  • 6.7 elaborate Yes/No questions with can,
  • 6.8 use can to ask questions about abilities.
• Adverbs of Manner quickly, slowly, carefully, beautifully, well.
✓ I can't run quickly. ✓ My sister can sing beautifully. ✓ Can you play well?
  • Identifying adverbs of manner.
  • Describing the way an activity is performed.
  • Writing short paragraphs about abilities.
  • 6.10 recognize adverbs of manner,
  • 6.11 express the way an activity is done,
  • 6.12 elaborate a short paragraph about abilities.
