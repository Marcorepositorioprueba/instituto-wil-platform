Unit 1: My History

15 Hours
1. Describe past activities and personal history. 2. Understand simple stories and biographies. 3. Use 'was/were' and 'did'.
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Past Simple (Be) Patterns: I was, You were, They weren't, Was he?

• Regular/Irregular Verbs eat-ate, go-went, watch-watched, study-studied.
A: Where were you yesterday? B: I was at school. A: What did you do? B: I studied for my exam.
  • Narrating past events chronologically.
  • Identifying irregular past forms.
  • Writing a simple biography.
  • Using time markers (yesterday, last week).
  • 1.1 describe past events using 'be',
  • 1.3 produce sentences with regular past endings,
  • 1.5 recall 30 high-frequency irregular verbs.

Unit 2: Health & Habits

15 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Body & Health headache, stomachache, cold, flu, fever.

• Modals for Advice Should / Shouldn't: You should see a doctor.
  • Giving and asking for health advice.
  • Roleplaying a doctor's visit.
  • Describing symptoms clearly.
  • 2.1 express physical discomfort accurately,
  • 2.4 use 'should' for appropriate suggestions,
  • 2.7 understand instructions for medication.

Unit 3: The City Navigator

18 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• City Places museum, library, bakery, mall, pharmacy.

• Directions & Movement go straight, turn left, across from, past the bank.
  • Giving detailed directions orally.
  • Reading a simple map of a foreign city.
  • Describing current city changes.
  • 3.1 identify 15 city landmarks,
  • 3.4 guide a peer through a physical map,
  • 3.6 use prepositions of place without errors.

Unit 4: Travel & Shopping

15 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Comparisons Comparative: taller than, better than, more expensive than. Superlative: the tallest, the best, the most expensive.
  • Comparing two products or cities.
  • Negotiating prices in a market simulation.
  • Expressing preferences in shopping.
  • 4.1 form comparatives for short/long adjectives,
  • 4.3 identify irregular comparison forms,
  • 4.5 describe the best features of a product.

Unit 5: Food Mastery

18 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Count/Uncount many, much, a few, a little, some, any.
A: Is there any milk? B: There is a little, but we need some apples.
  • Creating a shopping list for a recipe.
  • Ordering a multi-course meal in a restaurant.
  • Discussing dietary habits.
  • 5.2 distinguish countable/uncountable nouns,
  • 5.5 use quantifiers appropriately,
  • 5.8 interact fluently in food-service scenarios.

Unit 6: Future Horizons

15 Hours
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Future Plans Going to: for intentions. Will: for predictions.
A: What are you going to do tonight? B: I'm going to watch a movie.
  • Planning a trip with specific activities.
  • Predicting technological changes.
  • Expressing personal goals for the year.
  • 6.1 use 'going to' for personal intentions,
  • 6.4 make simple predictions about the future,
  • 6.7 write a 5-step personal growth plan.
