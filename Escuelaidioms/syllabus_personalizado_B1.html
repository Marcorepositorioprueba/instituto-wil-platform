Program Overview

Cognitive Goal

Independent communication in familiar matters (work, school, leisure).

Global Access

Ability to handle most situations while travelling in an area where English is spoken.

Production

Produce simple connected text on topics which are familiar or of personal interest.

Unit 1: Lifestyle & Experiences

15 Hours
1. Discuss life experiences and milestones. 2. Use Present Perfect vs Past Simple correctly.
Conceptual ContentProcedural ContentPerformance Indicators
• Perfect TensesPresent Perfect (Ever/Never/Just/Already/Yet). Duration with 'For' and 'Since'.
A: Have you ever been to London? B: Yes, I went there two years ago.
  • Narrating personal experiences.
  • Comparing life in the past and present.
  • Writing an informal blog post about a trip.
  • 1.1 describe experiences using Present Perfect,
  • 1.4 distinguish between specific and non-specific time.
